Privacy

Open PDF Library is a static website. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not require you to provide personal information to browse or read books.

Visitor statistics

This site uses a third-party visitor counter (FreeVisitorCounters) to display aggregate visit counts. That service may set cookies or collect standard web logs (such as IP address, browser type, and referring page) according to its own privacy practices. We do not control that service.

External links and PDFs

Some books may be linked from external hosts. When you open or download a file from another domain, that host’s privacy and logging practices apply.

Local storage

The site does not currently store personal data in your browser beyond what is needed for normal page operation.

If you have questions about privacy, please use the contact method described on the Disclaimer page.
